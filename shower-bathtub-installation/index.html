After Installation

What To Do To Prevent Water Damage And Mold

Check For Leaks In The First Week

Watch the area around the new fixture and the floor below for any sign of moisture during the first several uses.

Let Sealant Cure Fully

Avoid heavy water exposure on fresh caulk or grout until it's fully cured, per the product's stated cure time, to prevent early water intrusion behind the surround.

Ventilate The Bathroom

Running a fan or opening a window after showers helps prevent the humidity buildup that leads to mold in a newly finished space.

Report Anything Unusual Promptly

A soft spot in the floor or discoloration on a ceiling below the bathroom should be reported right away rather than monitored over time.

Design Considerations

Getting The Plumbing Right The First Time

Drain Placement

Converting a tub to a curbless shower often means relocating the drain, which is easier to plan before demolition than to correct afterward.

Valve Rough-In Height

Shower valve and showerhead height should be set for the actual household, not a generic default, before the wall is closed up.

Accessibility Features

Grab bar blocking and a bench seat, if planned, need proper wall support installed during the plumbing phase, not after tile is set.

Water Pressure Balance

A multi-head system needs properly sized supply lines so pressure doesn't drop noticeably when more than one head runs.

Older Fort Lauderdale homes undergoing a bathroom upgrade often reveal original galvanized or cast iron connections behind the existing tub or shower. That's worth planning for in the project budget rather than discovering as a surprise mid-renovation.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you handle the tile and surround work too?

We handle the plumbing scope. We can coordinate with a contractor for tile and surround work, or work alongside one you've already hired.

Will a tub-to-shower conversion require a permit?

Significant plumbing changes typically do, and we handle filing as part of the project.

What if you find old galvanized pipe behind the wall?

We'll explain what we found and quote any additional work needed before proceeding, rather than assuming the existing connections are fine.

How long does a typical conversion take?

The plumbing portion is often completed in a day, though the full project timeline depends on tile, surround, and other trades involved.

Can you add a bench seat or grab bars during the conversion?

Yes, and planning those features before the wall is closed up means proper blocking is installed for secure, lasting support.
