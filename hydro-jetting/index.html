When Jetting Is The Right Call

Beyond What A Snake Can Clear

Recurring Clogs

If the same drain clogs again within weeks of cabling, buildup further down the line is the likely cause.

Grease Buildup

Common in kitchen lines and commercial properties, grease hardens and narrows the pipe over time.

Root Intrusion

Mature tree roots common in Fort Lauderdale's older neighborhoods can be cut through and cleared with high-pressure water.

Pre-Sale Line Clearing

Some sellers jet the main line before listing a home to head off an inspection finding.

How It Compares

Jetting Vs. Standard Cabling

Full Diameter Cleaning

Jetting scours the entire pipe wall, not just a path through the clog like a cable does.

Longer-Lasting Result

Because it removes buildup rather than pushing it aside, jetting typically delays the next clog longer than cabling alone.

Root Cutting

High-pressure water combined with the right nozzle can cut through root intrusion at the source.

Pipe Condition Matters

Older, fragile pipe sometimes needs a gentler pressure setting, which we assess before jetting.

Process

How Hydro Jetting Works

Camera Inspection First

We check the line's condition and confirm jetting is appropriate before starting.

Set The Right Pressure

Pressure is calibrated to the pipe's age and material.

Jet The Line

High-pressure water scours the full pipe diameter clean.

Verify With Camera

A final camera pass confirms the line is fully clear.

Fort Lauderdale's older neighborhoods, with mature tree canopy over aging clay and cast iron sewer lines, see genuinely more root-related repeat clogs than newer, less-landscaped areas, which is exactly the profile where jetting tends to outperform standard cabling long-term. Commercial kitchens along Las Olas and downtown corridors also rely on regular jetting to keep grease trap lines flowing between scheduled pump-outs.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipe?

Yes, when pressure is calibrated for the pipe's age and condition, which we confirm with a camera inspection before jetting.

How is this different from a standard drain cleaning?

Standard cabling punches through a clog; jetting scours the entire pipe wall clean, which is why it tends to last longer on recurring issues.

Can jetting damage my pipes?

Not when pressure is properly calibrated. We check pipe condition first and adjust the approach for older or more fragile lines.

Do you offer jetting for commercial kitchens?

Yes, jetting is a common part of grease trap and kitchen line maintenance for restaurants and commercial properties.

How often should a commercial kitchen line be jetted?

It depends on kitchen volume and grease trap size, but many commercial clients set up a recurring schedule rather than waiting for a backup to happen.

Will jetting fix a fully collapsed pipe?

No, jetting clears buildup inside an intact pipe. A collapsed section needs repair or replacement instead, which we'll confirm with a camera before recommending jetting.
