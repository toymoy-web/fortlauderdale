About Plantation, FL

Real Local Facts

  • Named after the Everglades Plantation Company, which once owned much of the land; agricultural roots date to the 1940s.
  • Runs its own City of Plantation Utilities Department for water and wastewater service.
  • Plantation Acres is a genuine equestrian neighborhood with spacious lots and horse properties.
  • Plantation Isles sits along the South New River Canal, a waterfront community with private docks.

Housing & Plumbing Profile

A genuinely varied housing mix: equestrian-zoned Plantation Acres with large lots on the city's edges, waterfront Plantation Isles along the South New River Canal, and denser neighborhoods like Jacaranda and Jacaranda Lakes toward the center. Plantation runs its own water and wastewater utility, separate from Fort Lauderdale's system, which we factor into water quality and pressure diagnosis.

Plantation's own Utilities Department handles water and wastewater service citywide, separate from Fort Lauderdale's system, which matters when diagnosing a water quality or pressure complaint here. The city's canal network, a legacy of its agricultural past, also means waterfront properties along Plantation Isles and the South New River Canal get the same drainage and backflow consideration we apply to canal-adjacent lots elsewhere in our service area. If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Plantation

Shut Off The Main Water Valve

Stopping water at the source limits damage while help is on the way.

Cut Power To Wet Areas

If water is near an outlet or panel, switch off power to that section from a dry spot, only if it's safe.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move furniture, electronics, and belongings away from standing or spreading water.
