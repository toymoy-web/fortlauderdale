About Wilton Manors, FL

Real Local Facts

  • Known as the Island City, since the South and North Forks of the Middle River border the city on two sides.
  • Incorporated in 1947, covering just under 2 square miles, one of Broward's most compact cities.
  • Wilton Drive is the city's Arts and Entertainment District, a walkable commercial corridor.
  • Nine city parks, including two waterfront preserves along the Middle River.

Housing & Plumbing Profile

A compact, mostly built-out city where a meaningful share of properties sit close to the Middle River's two forks, which is relevant to drainage, backflow risk, and exterior plumbing on canal-adjacent lots. The mix runs from older homes near Wilton Drive to updated properties throughout the city's nine-park, two-square-mile footprint.
