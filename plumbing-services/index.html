Pricing Philosophy

Why Every Job Gets A Flat Price

A flat price means you know the cost before a wrench touches anything, not after. We inspect the issue, explain what we found, and quote a price you approve before work starts. If the scope changes once we're inside a wall or under a slab, we stop and requote rather than padding the invoice at the end.

No Surprise Invoices

The price you approve on site is the price you pay, barring a genuinely different scope discovered mid-repair.

Licensed And Insured

Every technician is licensed under Florida's plumbing framework and covered by insurance for your protection.

Warranty On Workmanship

Repairs are backed by a workmanship warranty, separate from any manufacturer warranty on parts.

Honest Repair-Or-Replace Advice

When a repair costs nearly as much as replacement, we say so rather than defaulting to the more expensive option.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Are your technicians actually licensed?

Yes, every technician is licensed under Florida's plumbing framework, and we're happy to confirm license and insurance details when you call.

Licensing & Compliance

What Backs Every Job

All work performed follows the plumbing provisions of the Florida Building Code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Gas line work follows the fuel gas provisions based on NFPA 54, the National Fuel Gas Code. Permits are filed whenever the scope of work requires one, and we don't cut that corner to save time.
