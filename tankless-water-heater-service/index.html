Common Tankless Issues

What Usually Brings A Service Call

Error Codes

Tankless units display specific codes for ignition, flow, or sensor issues that point directly to the cause.

Reduced Flow Or Temperature

Mineral scale buildup inside the heat exchanger is the most common cause in South Florida's water.

Cold Water Sandwich

A brief burst of cold water between hot water draws is a known tankless quirk, often solvable with a setting adjustment.

Unit Won't Ignite

Can be a gas supply issue, a venting problem, or a failed ignition component.

Maintenance That Matters

Keeping A Tankless Unit Running Well

Annual Descaling

South Florida's mineral content builds scale inside the heat exchanger faster than in areas with softer water.

Vent Inspection

Blocked or damaged venting is a real safety issue and a common cause of error codes.

Filter Cleaning

The inlet water filter needs periodic cleaning to maintain proper flow.

Settings Check

Some units benefit from a settings review, especially after a power outage resets defaults.

Descaling

Why Fort Lauderdale Units Need It More Often

Mineral Content

South Florida's water carries enough mineral content to scale a tankless heat exchanger faster than in areas with naturally softer water.

Manufacturer Guidance

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling in hard water areas to maintain warranty coverage and performance.

Signs You're Overdue

Reduced hot water temperature or flow, especially during simultaneous use, often means scale buildup.

The Process

A descaling solution is circulated through the unit to dissolve mineral buildup, restoring flow and efficiency.

Coastal Fort Lauderdale homes see more scale buildup in tankless units than areas with softer municipal water. That's a real, measurable factor in why some manufacturers' warranty terms specifically require documented annual descaling in this region. Skipping that maintenance step can void coverage on a unit that otherwise would have been repaired for free. Installation and venting follow the water heater provisions of the Florida Building Code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be descaled?

Annually is the common manufacturer recommendation for areas with mineral content like South Florida's water supply.

Can you convert my tank water heater to tankless?

Yes, we assess venting, gas supply, and space requirements before recommending a conversion.

Why does my tankless unit run out of hot water sometimes?

Tankless units are flow-rate limited, not capacity limited, so multiple simultaneous high-demand fixtures can exceed a unit's rated output.

Is the cold water sandwich effect normal?

Yes, it's a known characteristic of tankless systems, and a technician can often minimize it with a settings adjustment.

Can I descale a tankless unit myself?

Some homeowners do attempt a DIY descaling kit, but a professional service also inspects venting, connections, and the heat exchanger for issues a DIY flush wouldn't catch.
