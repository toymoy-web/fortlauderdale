Common Causes Here

Why Fort Lauderdale Homes Flood

Hurricane Season Storm Surge

Low-lying waterfront neighborhoods see genuine storm surge risk during Atlantic hurricane season, June through November.

Canal Backflow

Fort Lauderdale's extensive canal network means heavy rain can push water back into low-elevation properties near the water.

Burst Or Failed Pipes

A sudden pipe failure inside the home can flood a space just as thoroughly as outside water intrusion.

Roof And Window Leaks

Wind-driven rain during a tropical storm can force water in through roof and window seals not built for that pressure.

What Happens If You Wait

Why Fast Response Matters

Mold Growth Begins Within 24-48 Hours

South Florida's humidity accelerates mold growth on wet materials faster than in drier climates.

Structural Materials Absorb Water

Drywall, subflooring, and framing absorb water over time, turning a cleanup job into a reconstruction job.

Electrical Hazards

Standing water near outlets or a panel is a real safety risk that needs professional assessment, not a DIY response.

Secondary Damage Spreads

Water migrates further than the visible wet area, which is why moisture mapping matters before drying begins.

While You Wait

What To Do Before We Arrive

Stay Out Of Standing Water Near Outlets

Do not enter a room with standing water if it's near an electrical outlet or panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker if you can do so safely from a dry location.

Avoid Contaminated Water

Water from a sewage backup or storm surge should be treated as contaminated. Do not touch it directly or let children or pets near it.

Move Valuables If Safe

If you can do so without wading through deep or electrically hazardous water, move furniture and belongings to a dry area.

Document Before Cleanup

Photos of the damage before anything is moved or cleaned help support your insurance claim.

What We Bring

Equipment Behind A Fast Response

Truck-Mounted Extraction

High-capacity extraction equipment removes standing water far faster than portable wet-vacs alone.

Moisture Meters

Penetrating and non-penetrating moisture meters confirm exactly which materials are wet, not just where water is visible.

Industrial Air Movers

High-velocity air movers speed evaporation from carpets, subflooring, and wall cavities.

Commercial Dehumidifiers

Removing moisture from the air itself prevents materials from reabsorbing what's already been dried out.

Fort Lauderdale's combination of hurricane-season storm surge risk, an extensive canal network prone to backflow during heavy rain, and a humid climate that accelerates mold growth makes fast water damage response genuinely more urgent here than in drier, inland parts of the country. Assessment and drying protocols follow IICRC S500, the industry-standard reference for water damage restoration.

FAQs

Common Questions

How fast do you respond to a water damage call?

We treat these as emergencies and dispatch as quickly as possible, day or night, including during active storms when it's safe to do so.

Will insurance cover this?

Sudden water damage from a covered event like a burst pipe or storm is typically covered. We document everything to support your specific claim.

How long does drying take?

Most structures take three to five days to fully dry, monitored with moisture readings rather than a fixed guess.

Can you help during an active hurricane?

We respond as soon as it's safe to do so, and can often begin water extraction once conditions allow safe access to your property.
