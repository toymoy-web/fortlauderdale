What We Handle

Plumbing Scope For A Remodel

Fixture Relocation

Moving a toilet, sink, or shower to a new layout position requires rerouting supply and drain lines.

Rough-In Plumbing

New construction or a gutted bathroom needs full rough-in before walls and flooring go in.

Vanity And Sink Plumbing

Supply and drain connections for a new vanity, including double-sink setups.

Wet Room Conversions

Curbless, fully waterproofed wet room layouts need specific drain slope and placement planning.

Planning Considerations

What Affects The Project

Existing Plumbing Location

Keeping fixtures near existing supply and drain lines keeps costs lower than a full layout change.

Older Home Plumbing

Homes with original galvanized or cast iron connections often need updates during a full remodel.

Venting Requirements

New fixture locations need proper venting, which affects what's possible within an existing wall or floor structure.

Permit And Inspection

A full bathroom remodel's plumbing work typically requires permits and inspection at multiple stages.

Popular Upgrades

What Fort Lauderdale Homeowners Are Adding

Double Vanity Conversions

Converting a single-sink vanity to a double often means adding a second drain and supply set, planned around existing wall framing.

Freestanding Tubs

A freestanding tub needs floor-mounted or wall-mounted fill valves positioned before flooring goes down.

Heated Floor Prep

Radiant floor heating systems need coordination with the plumbing rough-in to avoid conflicts with drain lines.

Smart Fixtures

Digital shower controls and smart toilets have specific rough-in and power requirements we plan for early in the project.

Older Fort Lauderdale homes in neighborhoods like Victoria Park and Colee Hammock often reveal original cast iron and galvanized plumbing once a bathroom remodel gets past the demolition stage. We plan for that possibility in the initial quote rather than treating it as a surprise change order later. All rough-in work follows the plumbing provisions of the Florida Building Code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you move my toilet to a different wall?

Yes, though it requires rerouting the drain line, which affects cost more than moving a sink or shower would.

Do you coordinate with our contractor or designer?

Yes, we regularly work alongside general contractors and designers, fitting our timeline into the broader project schedule.

What if you find old plumbing we didn't know about?

We explain what we found and quote any additional work before proceeding, rather than treating it as an unavoidable surprise charge.

How long does the plumbing portion typically take?

A rough-in for a single bathroom is often a day or two, with a separate final connection visit once finishes are complete.
