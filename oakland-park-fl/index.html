About Oakland Park, FL

Real Local Facts

  • Incorporated in 1929, after the short-lived city of Floranada was dissolved.
  • Purchases its treated water from the City of Fort Lauderdale, though a few neighborhoods (North Andrews Gardens, Lakeside) receive service directly from Broward County instead.
  • Home to Royal Palm Park and the 46-acre Easterlin Park urban wilderness area.
  • A mix of mid-century homes and newer infill construction, with ongoing stormwater infrastructure upgrades in several neighborhoods.

Housing & Plumbing Profile

A dense, mostly built-out city with a good share of homes dating to the mid-20th century, alongside newer construction and redevelopment near the Funky Buddha Brewery and North Ridge Shopping Center corridors. Because the city's own water system buys treated water from Fort Lauderdale while a few neighborhoods are served directly by Broward County or Fort Lauderdale itself, confirming which utility actually serves a given address is a real, practical first step, not an assumption.

Oakland Park's water setup is genuinely layered: most of the city buys treated water through its own utility billing from Fort Lauderdale, while North Andrews Gardens and Lakeside are billed directly by Broward County, and Twin Lakes South is served directly by Fort Lauderdale.
