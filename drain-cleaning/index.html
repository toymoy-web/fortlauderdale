Signs Your Drain Needs Attention

When A Slow Drain Becomes A Real Clog

Slow Draining Sink Or Tub

Water that takes longer than usual to clear is often the earliest sign of buildup.

Gurgling Sounds

A gurgle from a drain or toilet when another fixture runs means air is trapped by a partial blockage.

Repeated Clogs In One Fixture

The same drain clogging again within weeks usually means the problem is further down the line, not at the fixture.

Backup In Multiple Fixtures

More than one drain backing up at once often points to the main line, not an individual fixture.

Why Older Fort Lauderdale Homes Clog More

Cast Iron And Root Intrusion

Aging Cast Iron Lines

Homes in Victoria Park, Colee Hammock, and Rio Vista often still run original cast iron drain lines that narrow with age as the interior corrodes.

Root Intrusion

Mature trees common in these older neighborhoods can send roots into small cracks in older clay or cast iron sewer lines.

Grease Buildup

Grease poured down a kitchen drain hardens as it cools, narrowing the pipe over months.

Sand And Sediment

South Florida's sandy soil can work into an aging line through small cracks and settle as sediment.

Snaking Vs. Hydro Jetting

Which Method Actually Clears The Line

Standard Cabling (Snake)

  • Punches a hole through most clogs quickly.
  • Lower cost for a straightforward blockage.
  • Does not scour the pipe walls clean.
  • Grease and roots often return within months.

Hydro Jetting

  • High-pressure water scours the full pipe diameter.
  • Clears grease buildup a cable pushes through but leaves behind.
  • Cuts through root intrusion at the source.
  • Longer-lasting result on a repeat-clog line.

South Florida's sandy soil and the mature tree canopy in neighborhoods like Rio Vista and Colee Hammock make root intrusion a genuinely more common cause of repeat clogs here than in newer, treeless subdivisions further inland. Knowing that going in changes whether a snake is enough or whether jetting is worth doing the first time.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is hydro jetting safe for old cast iron pipe?

Yes, when pressure is set appropriately for the pipe's age and condition, which we assess before jetting.

Why does the same drain keep clogging?

A repeat clog in one fixture usually means the real blockage is further down the shared line, not at that fixture.

Can tree roots really get into a sewer line?

Yes, particularly through small existing cracks in older clay or cast iron pipe common in Fort Lauderdale's older neighborhoods.
