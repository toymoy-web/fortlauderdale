Broward County Compliance

Backflow And Grease Trap Requirements

Backflow Testing Schedule

Broward County commercial properties are commonly required to test backflow prevention assemblies annually. This follows the backflow provisions of the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted in the Florida Building Code, to protect the public water supply from contamination.

Grease Trap Compliance

Restaurants are typically required to keep grease trap service records on hand and pump on a schedule tied to trap size and kitchen volume.

Multi-Tenant Coordination

Work in a shared building often needs coordination with a property manager or building engineer, especially for riser or main shutoff work.

Documentation

We provide service documentation for your records, useful for insurance, lease compliance, or a future sale.

Las Olas Boulevard and downtown Fort Lauderdale pack a dense mix of restaurants, retail, and office space into a small footprint, which means a plumbing issue at one address can affect neighboring tenants faster than in a standalone suburban building. Scheduling service before a small issue becomes a shared one is usually the cheaper path for everyone involved, and it is worth building into a lease or property management plan rather than waiting for an emergency call.
