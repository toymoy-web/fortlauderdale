FAQs

Common Questions

Do you serve areas not listed here?

Our primary coverage is the cities listed on this page. If your address is nearby, call and we can confirm whether we're able to help.

Does response time vary by city?

Yes, distance from our Fort Lauderdale base affects drive time, though every city we list gets the same 24/7 availability.

Is pricing different depending on the city?

No, our flat-price-before-work standard applies the same way regardless of which city you're in.

Can I request a specific technician for my area?

We route technicians based on availability and location to get to you as quickly as possible, rather than assigning by request.

What if my city isn't listed but is close to one that is?

Call and give us your address. Coverage often extends slightly beyond the five cities listed, and we'll confirm honestly rather than guess.

Do you charge extra for a farther address within your coverage area?

No, our flat-price standard doesn't change based on distance within our normal coverage area. An after-hours emergency rate is disclosed up front regardless of location.

How do I know if my address qualifies as within your coverage area?

The simplest way is to call with your address. We'll confirm coverage immediately rather than making you guess from a map.

A Note On Coverage

Why We List Cities Individually

Rather than listing a generic mile radius, we built a dedicated page for each city we serve. Each one has real, checkable details, like which water utility that city actually uses and what its housing stock generally looks like. A plumber who knows that Pompano Beach runs its own water utility, separate from Fort Lauderdale's, or that western Davie has genuine well-and-septic properties, diagnoses problems faster than one working from a generic radius map.
