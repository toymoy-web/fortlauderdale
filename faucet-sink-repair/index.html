Common Faucet Issues

What Usually Brings A Repair Call

Constant Dripping

Almost always a worn washer, O-ring, or cartridge inside the faucet body.

Low Water Pressure At One Fixture

Often a clogged aerator, a quick and inexpensive fix compared to a pressure issue elsewhere in the home.

Loose Or Wobbly Faucet

A base that's come loose from the sink or counter needs resealing to prevent water getting underneath.

Sink Draining Slowly

Usually buildup in the trap or the line just past it, cleared without needing to touch the faucet itself.

Sink And Fixture Work

Beyond The Faucet Itself

P-Trap Leaks

A leaking trap under the sink is a common, low-cost fix that prevents cabinet water damage.

Garbage Disposal Interaction

Sink and disposal plumbing are connected, and a disposal issue can sometimes present as a sink drainage problem.

Supply Line Replacement

Old, brittle supply lines under a sink are worth replacing proactively before they fail.

Sink Replacement

When a sink itself is cracked or outdated, we can handle full replacement alongside faucet work.

South Florida's water carries enough mineral content to accelerate wear on faucet cartridges and aerators faster than in some other regions. That's part of why a faucet that's only a few years old can still develop a drip sooner than its expected lifespan would suggest. Regularly cleaning the aerator can slow this wear, though it won't stop a cartridge that's already failing. FAQs

Common Questions

Can you match my existing faucet finish?

In most cases yes, though for an exact match on an older or discontinued finish we'll confirm availability before ordering.

Is a dripping faucet really worth fixing right away?

Yes, a persistent drip adds up on your water bill and often gets worse rather than resolving on its own.

Do you replace supply lines during a faucet repair?

We'll flag it if the existing lines look worn and offer to replace them at the same visit to avoid a future leak.

Can you fix a wobbly bathroom sink faucet?

Yes, a loose base is a common and quick fix that also prevents water from getting underneath and causing further damage.

Why does my new-ish faucet already have a drip?

Mineral content in the local water supply wears cartridges faster than in some regions, so a faucet under warranty sometimes needs a cartridge swap well before the unit itself is due for replacement.
