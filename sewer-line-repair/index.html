Signs Of A Damaged Sewer Line

When It's The Main Line, Not A Single Drain

Multiple Drains Backing Up At Once

More than one fixture backing up together, especially the lowest drain in the house, points to the main line.

Sewage Odor In The Yard

A persistent sewage smell outside, especially over one specific area of lawn, often points to a break underground.

Unusually Green Or Lush Patch Of Grass

A section of yard growing noticeably better than the rest can mean a leaking sewer line is fertilizing it.

Gurgling From Toilets

Toilets that gurgle when a washing machine or dishwasher drains suggest an obstruction further down the shared line.

Common Causes Here

Why Fort Lauderdale Sewer Lines Fail

Root Intrusion

Mature trees in older neighborhoods like Victoria Park and Colee Hammock send roots into small cracks in aging clay or cast iron pipe.

Pipe Age

Original sewer lines installed decades ago are approaching or past their expected service life in many historic neighborhoods.

Ground Shifting

Sandy South Florida soil can shift over time, offsetting pipe joints and creating a low spot that traps waste.

Grease And Debris Buildup

Years of grease and debris narrow a line gradually until a partial clog becomes a full backup.

FAQs

Common Questions

Will you dig up my whole yard?

Not necessarily. We check whether trenchless lining or a targeted spot repair is possible before recommending full excavation.

How do you know it's the sewer line and not a fixture?

A camera inspection confirms the exact location and nature of the blockage or damage rather than guessing from symptoms alone.

Can tree roots really break through pipe?

Yes, especially through small existing cracks or joints in older clay or cast iron pipe common in Fort Lauderdale's established neighborhoods.

What if the damage is under my driveway?

We assess whether trenchless lining can reach the damaged section without disturbing the driveway before defaulting to excavation.

Is a sewer line repair urgent or can it wait?

A slow-draining line rarely improves on its own and tends to progress to a full backup, so we recommend addressing it before it becomes an emergency call.
