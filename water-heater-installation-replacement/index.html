Signs It's Time

When Repair Stops Making Sense

No Hot Water

A unit that can't keep up with demand or produces no hot water at all is usually past a simple fix.

Rusty Or Discolored Water

Discoloration from the hot tap often means internal tank corrosion that a repair won't reverse.

Popping Or Rumbling Noises

Sediment buildup rumbling as the unit heats is common in Fort Lauderdale's harder well-sourced water areas.

Age Past 10-12 Years

A tank water heater in a coastal, humid climate often needs replacement sooner than the same unit would inland.

Tank Vs. Tankless

Which Fits Your Fort Lauderdale Home

Traditional Tank

  • Lower upfront cost.
  • Simpler installation in most homes.
  • Limited hot water supply during heavy use.
  • 10-15 year lifespan, often shorter near the coast.

Tankless

  • Endless hot water on demand.
  • Lower monthly energy cost over time.
  • Compact wall-mounted unit frees floor space.
  • 20+ year lifespan with proper maintenance.
Before We Arrive

Water Heater Safety Checklist

Cut Power Or Gas First

If you smell gas or the unit is sparking, shut off the gas supply or breaker before doing anything else, and leave the area if the gas smell is strong.

Don't Touch A Wet Electric Unit

Never touch an electric water heater or its breaker while standing in water on the floor around it.

Know Your Shutoff Valve

Locate the cold water shutoff valve at the top of the tank so you can stop the water supply if it's actively leaking.

Ventilate Gas Units

Never block the venting on a gas water heater, and open a window if you notice a gas odor while waiting for us to arrive.

Homes closer to the Intracoastal and canal-front neighborhoods tend to see faster tank corrosion than inland Broward County properties.
