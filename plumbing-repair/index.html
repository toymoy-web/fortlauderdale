Common Problems

Repairs We Handle Most Often In Fort Lauderdale

Running Or Leaking Toilet

A toilet that runs constantly or leaks at the base usually means a worn flapper, fill valve, or wax ring.

Dripping Faucets

A slow drip is almost always a worn washer or cartridge, an inexpensive fix if caught early.

Low Water Pressure

Older galvanized or cast iron lines in Victoria Park and Colee Hammock corrode from the inside. This narrows the pipe and cuts pressure over time.

Unexplained Water Bill Spike

A sudden jump in your bill with no new usage often points to a hidden leak somewhere in the line.

Temporary Fix Vs. Professional Repair

Why A Quick Fix Often Costs More Later

Store-Bought Sealant Or Tape

  • Buys a few days at most on a real leak.
  • Does not address a corroded or cracked fitting.
  • Can trap moisture and hide worsening damage.
  • Often needs to be redone within weeks.

Diagnosed Repair

  • Identifies the actual failure point first.
  • Matches the fix to the pipe material, cast iron included.
  • Pressure-tested before we leave.
  • Backed by a workmanship warranty on the repair.

Fort Lauderdale's building stock spans nearly a century, from 1920s bungalows near downtown to condos built in the last decade. The right repair approach depends heavily on which era of plumbing is actually behind the wall. A technician checks the pipe material before quoting. That avoids the common mistake of pricing a modern PEX repair the same as a cast iron one. All repair work follows the Florida Building Code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you fix a leak without cutting into a wall?

Often yes. We use electronic leak detection first to pinpoint the source before any wall or slab is opened.

Do you work on older cast iron plumbing?

Yes, including partial repairs and section replacements in Victoria Park, Colee Hammock, and Rio Vista homes.

What if the repair turns out bigger than expected?

We stop and requote before doing any additional work beyond what you approved.

Do you repair PEX and PVC as well as older pipe?

Yes. Most newer Fort Lauderdale construction runs PEX or PVC. We carry fittings for both, plus the older materials still common in historic neighborhoods.
