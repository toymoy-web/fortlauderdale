What We're Actually Looking For

Root Intrusion

Roots entering through small cracks or joints, a common issue in tree-lined older neighborhoods.

Pipe Material And Condition

Whether the line is original clay, cast iron, or already-updated PVC, and its current condition.

Bellies Or Sagging

A section that has settled and holds standing water rather than draining fully.

Offsets Or Cracks

Shifted joints or cracks that let debris or roots enter, or let sewage escape into the surrounding soil.

Process

How A Camera Inspection Works

Access The Line

We locate an existing cleanout or access point, no digging required.

Run The Camera

A waterproof camera feeds real-time video as it travels the length of the line.

Document Findings

We record footage and note the location of any issues found.

Recommend Next Steps

You get an honest recommendation, whether that's no action needed or a specific repair.

Many homes in Fort Lauderdale's older neighborhoods have never had their original sewer line inspected, and a camera pass is often the only way to know whether a line that's been fine for decades is starting to show real wear. Buyers looking at homes in Victoria Park, Colee Hammock, and Rio Vista specifically should expect original clay or cast iron lines and budget an inspection into the closing timeline rather than assuming everything below ground is fine. Findings are documented using the defect-coding conventions from NASSCO's Pipeline Assessment and Certification Program (PACP), the industry-standard framework for grading sewer line condition, and any recommended repair follows the Florida Building Code's plumbing provisions, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

A camera inspection itself is not typically covered by homeowner's insurance, since it's a diagnostic service rather than a repair for sudden damage. If the inspection uncovers a covered failure, keep the footage and report, since insurers often ask for documentation of the actual condition found.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I get a copy of the footage?

Yes, recorded footage and notes on any issues found are provided after the inspection.

Can this replace a home inspector's report?

It's a useful supplement focused specifically on the sewer line, not a replacement for a full home inspection.

What happens if you find a problem?

We explain what we found and quote repair options separately, with no pressure to decide on the spot.

How long does an inspection take?

Most single-family home inspections take under an hour, depending on line length and how many access points need to be checked.

Can you inspect a line with an active clog?

Yes, though we typically clear the worst of the blockage first so the camera can travel the full length of the line.
