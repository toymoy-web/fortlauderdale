About Davie, FL

Real Local Facts

  • Originally settled in the early 1900s as a farming community once known as Zona; renamed Davie in 1916, incorporated in 1961.
  • Retains genuine equestrian zoning on thousands of parcels, concentrated in western Davie, with lots often an acre or larger.
  • Home to Nova Southeastern University's main campus and part of Broward College.
  • Land was originally drained Everglades wetland/farmland, relevant to soil and drainage conditions on larger lots.

Housing & Plumbing Profile

A genuine split between dense, newer subdivisions near the universities and large-acreage equestrian properties in western Davie, some still on well and septic rather than municipal water and sewer. The town's early-1900s agricultural roots are still visible in some of these larger, older western parcels, a detail that shapes how we plan a service visit and how much exterior plumbing a given property is likely to have.
