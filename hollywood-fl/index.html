About Hollywood, FL

Real Local Facts

  • Founded in 1925 by California developer Joseph W. Young as a planned 'Dream City,' with a radial boulevard grid centered on Young Circle.
  • The Great Hurricane of September 1926 caused major damage during the city's early development.
  • Hollywood Beach Broadwalk is a 2.5-mile beachfront promenade lined with Streamline/Art Moderne and Mediterranean Revival buildings dating to the late 1920s-1930s.
  • Hollywood Lakes and Hollywood Hills/Emerald Hills are real, distinct historic and golf-course neighborhoods west and near downtown.
  • Roughly 80 percent of Port Everglades sits within Hollywood city limits.

FAQs

Common Questions From Hollywood Residents

Is this Hollywood, Florida or Hollywood, California?

Florida. We serve Hollywood, FL in Broward County, not the California city of the same name.

Do you service the historic Hollywood Lakes neighborhood?

Yes, we service Hollywood Lakes alongside the rest of the city, including beachfront properties along the Broadwalk.

What plumbing issues are common in Hollywood's 1920s beachfront buildings?

Buildings from the late 1920s and 1930s era near the Broadwalk often still have original supply lines, which carry the same salt-air corrosion considerations as Fort Lauderdale's own historic coastal neighborhoods.

Do you service Hollywood Hills and Emerald Hills too?

Yes, we cover those golf-course communities further inland alongside the beachfront and downtown areas.

Does Hollywood have its own water utility separate from Fort Lauderdale?

Yes, the City of Hollywood operates its own municipal water utility serving the city's roughly 30 square miles and over 153,000 residents.

Do you service properties near Young Circle and downtown Hollywood?

Yes, downtown Hollywood and the area around Young Circle are within our regular Hollywood, FL coverage.

How far is Hollywood, FL from your base in Fort Lauderdale?

About 7.7 miles, which keeps it within our standard same-day and emergency response radius.
